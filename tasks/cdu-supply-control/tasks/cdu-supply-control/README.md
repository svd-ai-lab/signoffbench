## Difficulty explanation

Coordinate three pumps and a valve to maintain rack flow, condensation margin and pressure limits under sensor drift, power loss and rack-service changes. The engineer must diagnose inconsistent observations and balance coupled thermal and hydraulic constraints.

A CDU controls/commissioning engineer must release a fault-tolerant controller for a liquid-cooled server row. Hydraulic flow, differential pressure and absolute pressure constrain the same pump commands; cooling must meet both an upper supply-temperature limit and a dew-point floor. Redundant sensors can disagree because of drift, noise, flatline or open circuits, while pump trips, unequal sharing and a sticking valve change the plant response. The controller must diagnose these conditions from 23 observations, preserve state across scans and coordinate four outputs without a fault schedule or case identity.

The plant is an author-developed Simulink engineering model, exported as FMI 3.0 Co-Simulation FMUs. Qualification fault schedules and equipment variations are author-created simulations, not recorded field failures. Published vendor/OCP engineering material informs the plant and qualification bounds; training-load shapes are derived from the Ahmed-Elsayed95 high-resolution AI training workload dataset (revision `f7f8d62e806ce2b64e93c58fce2bb18604f067ad`), with Meta's Llama 3 paper used only as training-synchronization background, while load-bank steps are constructed commissioning inputs. Source descriptions and limitations are in the [equipment documents](environment/work/docs/), [qualification scope](environment/work/docs/qualification.md) and [vendor context](solution/vendor-context.md). The category is Science / Physics and the technical direction is dynamical systems and feedback control.

## Solution explanation

The reference ports a stateful PLC scan. It checks redundant RTDs for range, flatline, noise and persistent disagreement, and selects differential pressure using the two transmitters and the pressure difference. Pump management combines standby staging, trip feed-forward, stage-in back-off, degradation detection and flow-sharing trim. A differential-pressure PI loop includes a supply-pressure override. The valve loop combines an approach-temperature target with a dew-point floor, flow feed-forward, conditional integration and seized-valve handling.

These mechanisms coordinate transient flow recovery with pressure limits and prevent integrator wind-up from driving temperature below the condensation margin. They are one feasible strategy, not a required implementation. The Python reference sees the same declared observations as a submission. The runtime owns the 0.5-second scan and one-scan actuator delay; the reference does not add another delay. The historical model validation matrix is included in [review/independent-controller-results.json](review/independent-controller-results.json).

## Verification explanation

Each configured case starts a new controller process and instance; reset is called before stepping, including a repeated pre-step reset. Cases do not reuse an instance after stepping, so the harness does not claim to test post-use reset semantics. Only the four declared actuator commands may be returned. Interface exceptions, non-finite outputs, premature exits and invalid submissions fail execution independently of physical acceptance.

A separate verifier assesses native plant truth at one-second samples from 300 through 1200 seconds inclusive. In-service rack groups have isolation fraction at least 0.999. Flow deficit below 90% of 125 GPM is normalized per group, summed over samples and maximized across groups. The other exposure metrics sum positive violations of dew point + 2 K, 55 C supply temperature, 120 psig supply pressure and 95 psid differential pressure. Each exposure must be zero. The starvation metric is the longest consecutive in-service run below 50% of design flow and must be less than 30 samples. All six items must pass every configured case for reward 1; otherwise reward is 0.

Engineering requirements come from the stated commissioning contract and its documented qualification bounds, not from tuning to reference success. The reference runs establish feasibility; the fixed five independent model runs supply the current difficulty evidence and include two fully passing alternative implementations. [Acceptance basis](review/acceptance-basis.md) explains each bound, the modeled variation and why zero violation does not require agreement with a reference trajectory. The [five-controller matrix](review/independent-controller-results.json) and [13 metric boundary probes](review/boundary-probes.json) provide local evidence. The matrix records historical submissions, not new runs of the revised instruction. Acceptance-profile calibration labels are historical model-development metadata.

The candidate-facing interface JSON lists four commands. The verifier FMU metadata also lists five legacy alarm inputs: the runtime initializes and holds those at zero, and accepts only the four actuator commands from a submission. Both trees use the same actuator order and physical FMUs; the metadata distinction does not expose additional candidate controls. See [controller interface](tests/work/runtime/controller_interface.py) and [FMU runtime](tests/work/runtime/run_fmu.py).

The verifier writes report.json, binary reward, CTRF check results and per-case metrics/provenance/sampling summaries. Full time series are used for scoring and finite-value validation, then omitted from persisted case files. The case inventory, task snapshot and submitted file are frozen per evaluation; reports identify the exact evaluated version. Unprivileged controller processes cannot access the root-only tests or reward directories. The live scan protocol uses JSON lines. The verifier captures stdout and stderr in unlinked root-created temporary files, reads stdout without blocking on EOF, and retains at most a 1 MiB pending protocol line and a 64 KiB diagnostic tail. Writes to the stdin pipe are nonblocking; both send and receive operations respect the 900-second verifier time budget. Each controller starts a new session and receives a unique per-case UID. At case closure the verifier kills the process group and any detached descendants with that UID before closing the input stream and capture files. A root supervisor bounds the complete verifier run. [Transport regression evidence](review/controller-transport-validation.json) covers stalled and detached children, malformed output, backpressure and six complete native candidate replays with unchanged physical metrics.
